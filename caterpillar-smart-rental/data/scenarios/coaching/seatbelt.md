# Operator Safety Coaching — Seatbelt Compliance

**Issue detected:** Seatbelt not fastened while the machine was operating.

**Why it matters:** Heavy equipment can move, stop, or change direction unexpectedly. Uneven ground and sudden machine movement can increase injury risk. The operator restraint system is intended to help keep the operator properly positioned.

**Correct operating behaviour**
- Sit correctly in the operator seat.
- Fasten the seatbelt securely before operation.
- Keep the seatbelt fastened while operating.
- If the restraint does not latch or appears damaged, report it and follow site procedures before operating.

**Immediate action**
1. Stop the machine safely when conditions permit.
2. Fasten the seatbelt before resuming operation.
3. Verify the restraint is functioning correctly.
4. Acknowledge the safety event and complete coaching for repeated violations.

**Quick safety checklist**
- [ ] Operator correctly seated
- [ ] Seatbelt fastened
- [ ] Restraint latches correctly
- [ ] Operator is authorized to operate
- [ ] Work area and operating conditions checked
- [ ] Site safety procedures followed

Telemetry alerts support safety awareness; they do not replace operator training, site procedures, inspections, or supervisor instructions.
